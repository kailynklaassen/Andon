## 1. Executive summary

Q3 FY26 was a quarter of divergence. Wroclaw and Pune stayed at or close to their year-to-date levels, but Columbus, Greenville and Monterrey fell further from the 85% corporate OEE target. Two causes explain most of the gap: the failing spindle on GVL-L3-WC01 at Greenville (an equipment reliability issue) and the deterioration of castings supply from Brightline Castings (SUP-1007) at Monterrey and Columbus (a supply issue). A third issue, night-shift crimp defects at Wroclaw after the 2026-06-08 die change, began at the end of the quarter and is not yet visible in OEE. No plant reached the corporate target in the quarter.

OEE is measured per SOP-PRD-001: Availability x Performance x Quality, on a 450-minute planned shift.

## 4. Plant highlights

### 4.1 Columbus (CLB) - the benchmark
Plant manager Kevin Thompson. Columbus achieved 96.6% PM compliance for FY26 against a 97% target and has the lowest breakdown rate and highest OEE of the US plants. Practices: condition-based PM triggers set at OEM limits, a weekly reliability stand-up, and a spare-spindle program for A-critical CNCs. Q3 losses came mainly from castings shortages on CLB-L1 from Brightline. See case study CS-CLB-2026-02.

### 4.2 Greenville (GVL) - spindle failure
Plant manager Tom Brennan. GVL-L3 OEE fell from 84% in March to 76% in April, 67% in May and 60% in June. Vibration on GVL-L3-WC01 (Hartwell HAR-119) passed the OEM 4.5 mm/s replacement limit in June while the internal PM-CNC-030 Rev C limit of 7.1 mm/s kept PMs from triggering an action. CAPA-2026-103 (bore position drift) was opened 2026-05-24 and is due 2026-07-23. MOC-2026-031 to correct the PM limit was submitted in July and is awaiting engineering approval. A replacement cartridge has a 10-week lead time and a quoted $68,400 installed price; there is no spare on site.

### 4.3 Monterrey (MTY) - material shortages
Plant manager Alejandro Reyes. From 2026-05-01 Brightline Castings (Saltillo) changed its furnace line and sand supplier. On-time delivery fell from 92% to 44% with 9.3 days average lateness when late; incoming quality rose from 237 PPM to 2,726 PPM, against the 500 PPM limit in SQA-BLC-2023. Material Shortage downtime rose on MTY-L1 and MTY-L2; see LSR-MTY-2026-011 (MTY-L1, June 2026). SCAR-2026-014 was issued on 2026-05-25 and tracked as CAPA-2026-101, due 2026-07-24. A dual-sourcing proposal with Keystone Foundry (SUP-1002) for MAT-5001 and MAT-5002 is under review (PPAP about 12 weeks, +6% piece price). Quality manager: Guadalupe Flores. Supplier Quality Engineer: Rosa Martinez.

### 4.4 Wroclaw (WRO) - night-shift quality
Plant manager Katarzyna Nowak. OEE and PM compliance (97.9%) are strong, but after a crimp die change on WRO-L1-WC02 on 2026-06-08 the in-process defect rate on C shift reached 13.6% against about 1.4-1.5% on A and B shifts. The 8D (8D-WRO-2026-009) points to use of superseded work instruction WI-WRO-CR-07 Rev D and the lack of a Level 3 certified setter on C shift. CAPA-2026-102 was opened 2026-07-08 (due 2026-08-22; In Progress). Quality engineer Tomasz Wisniewski; C-shift supervisor Marek Kaminski.

### 4.5 Pune (PNQ)
Pune posted the highest OEE at 83.8% for the quarter, with PM compliance of 96.0% for the year. Losses are mostly press die changeovers on PNQ-L2.
