## 3. Demand Review

3.1 Demand for agriculture and construction OEMs remains firm through the fourth quarter of FY26. Order intake is in line with the plan; no downward revisions were requested.

3.2 Gearbox demand from Strategic OEM customers, including CUST-2001 Apex Agricultural Equipment, is above what Greenville can currently produce. Open backlog on gearbox assemblies is growing.

3.3 Rebecca Haines of Apex sent an escalation letter on 2026-08-18 on late deliveries; the response was issued on 2026-08-26 (ESC-APEX-2026-08).

## 4. Supply Review

### 4.1 Gearbox constraint – Greenville (GVL-L3)

- GVL-L3 Gearbox Assembly is capacity-constrained by GVL-L3-WC01 (CNC Horizontal Machining Center #1), the line bottleneck with criticality A.
- July line OEE was 55% (against a 60% June). Spindle bearing vibration was above the OEM replace limit of 4.5 mm/s from June and has continued to rise.
- Tom Brennan reported repeated unplanned stops from spindle bearing wear. The replacement spindle cartridge has a **10-week lead time** from Hartwell Machine Tools and no spare is on site; the installed quote is $68,400.
- Pending change MOC-2026-031 (align PM-CNC-030 with the OEM limit) is awaiting engineering approval. CAPA-2026-103 (bore position drift) remains open and overdue.
- Gearbox output is expected to remain constrained until the cartridge is installed. Supply Chain will protect customer commitments by prioritizing Strategic customers' allocations.

### 4.2 Castings constraint – Brightline Castings (SUP-1007)

- Brightline on-time delivery is 44% since 1 May (92% before) and incoming PPM is 2,726 (limit 500). SCAR-2026-014 / CAPA-2026-101 is overdue.
- Material-shortage stops continue on GVL-L3, MTY-L3, CLB-L2 and MTY-L1. Alejandro Reyes and Kevin Thompson reported resequencing to protect the highest-priority orders.
- Rosa Martinez confirmed Brightline has not submitted an acceptable permanent corrective action.
- Priya Nair presented the outline of the dual-sourcing plan (to be formalized as PRP-SC-2026-04): Keystone Foundry (SUP-1002) for MAT-5001/MAT-5002, approximately 12 weeks PPAP and +6% piece price; Silesia Metalworks (SUP-1003) as a European option.

### 4.3 Inventory

Raw-material days of supply for castings at WH-MTY and WH-CLB are below the 35-day target; the temporary 21-day safety stock for Brightline-sourced items (POL-INV-001 section 3.3) is in force. Planners will buy forward on available Keystone capacity for MAT-5003.

## 5. Logistics Review

5.1 Ridgeway Freight (CAR-04) on-time was 74% in July and is 72% month to date for August against a 95% SLA in CTA-RF-2024-07. It carries about 38% of DC-MEM shipments.

5.2 Brian Wilson reported that Apex shipments will be moved to Summit Logistics (CAR-01) and BlueLine Express (CAR-02) effective 2026-09-01, in line with the Apex response plan.

5.3 Service credits under clause 4.1 of CTA-RF-2024-07 will be claimed; the Memphis carrier review (CPR-MEM-2026-08) will quantify them.

5.4 Premium freight spend is elevated due to castings shortages and late carrier deliveries. Approvals follow POL-INV-001 section 7.
