## 1. Parties and Purpose

1.1 This Agreement is between KK's Parts ("Shipper") and Ridgeway Freight ("Carrier") for less-than-truckload (LTL) transportation of finished goods from the Memphis Distribution Center (DC-MEM) to Shipper's customers.

1.2 Carrier is identified in Shipper's systems as CAR-04, mode LTL.

## 2. Lanes and Services

2.1 The lanes below are covered. Planned transit days are agreed per lane and are the basis for the carrier due date (clause 3.2).

| Lane | Origin | Destination region | Planned transit days |
|---|---|---|---|
| MEM-MW | DC-MEM | Midwest (IL, IN, IA, MN, WI) | 2 |
| MEM-SE | DC-MEM | Southeast (GA, AL, NC, SC, FL) | 2 |
| MEM-SW | DC-MEM | Southwest (TX, OK, AR, LA) | 2 |
| MEM-NE | DC-MEM | Northeast and Mid-Atlantic | 4 |
| MEM-MT | DC-MEM | Mountain and Plains | 4 |

2.2 Pickup is scheduled daily at the DC-MEM outbound dock. Carrier shall furnish a tracking number (PRO number) at pickup and provide status events electronically (EDI 214) at pickup, in transit, out for delivery and delivered.

## 3. Service Level Agreement

3.1 **On-time SLA.** Carrier shall deliver at least **95%** of shipments on time in each calendar month.

3.2 **Definition.** A shipment is on time if it is delivered on or before the **carrier due date**, defined as the **ship date plus planned transit days** for the lane. Delivery before the due date is on time. Delivery after the due date is late, regardless of customer-requested date.

3.3 Excluded from measurement: shipments delayed by Shipper (appointment refusal, incorrect address), force majeure events declared by federal or state authorities, and customer-requested holds documented in the shipment record.

3.4 Performance is calculated from Shipper's shipment records, reconciled monthly with Carrier's EDI 214 delivered events. Disputes must be raised within 15 days of the monthly report.

## 4. Service Credits

4.1 If monthly on-time performance falls below **92%**, Carrier shall issue a service credit equal to **2% of that month's freight spend with Carrier for each percentage point below 92%**.

4.2 Credits are calculated on whole points, applied against the next invoice, or paid within 30 days if the Agreement has ended. Credits are Shipper's remedy for late delivery in the measured month and do not limit rights under clause 8.

4.3 Worked example: monthly on-time of 80% is 12 points below 92%, so the credit is 24% of that month's Ridgeway freight spend.

4.4 Performance between 92% and 95% does not generate a credit but triggers a corrective-action discussion at the next business review (clause 6).

## 5. Rates and Accessorials

5.1 Base rates are per hundredweight by lane and weight break and are held for the term, subject to the annual fuel-surcharge table (Schedule B). Detention, liftgate and residential delivery accessorials are payable only when authorized on the bill of lading.

5.2 Premium or expedited service requested by Shipper is quoted per shipment and must be approved by the DC-MEM Logistics Manager.

## 6. Business Reviews

6.1 The parties shall hold a quarterly business review (QBR) attended by Shipper's Director of Supply Chain & Logistics and Memphis Logistics Manager and Carrier's account manager.

6.2 Each QBR covers monthly on-time performance by lane, credits issued, claims and damage rates, driver and capacity outlook for peak season, and the status of open corrective actions.

6.3 If monthly on-time is below the SLA for two consecutive months, Carrier shall present a written corrective action plan within 10 business days.

## 7. Claims and Liability

7.1 Carrier's liability for loss or damage is the released value stated on the bill of lading, not to exceed the limits in Schedule C. Claims must be filed within 9 months of delivery.

7.2 Carrier shall carry cargo liability insurance of not less than USD 250,000 per occurrence and auto liability of not less than USD 1,000,000.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 The initial term is three years from 2024-07-01 and renews annually unless either party gives notice of non-renewal 90 days before expiry.

8.2 **Termination for convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on **60 days' written notice**. Shipper has no minimum volume commitment, and may reallocate lane volume to other carriers at any time without penalty.

8.3 Shipper may terminate on 15 days' notice for repeated SLA failure, defined as monthly on-time below 92% in three consecutive months, after the corrective action process in clause 6.3 has been invoked.

## 9. Data and Systems

9.1 Carrier shall transmit EDI 214 status messages within 4 hours of each event. Duplicate transmissions are permitted only if they carry the same shipment identifier and event timestamp, so that Shipper can de-duplicate them (see SOP-WMS-002).

9.2 Shipper may audit Carrier delivery records on 10 business days' notice.
