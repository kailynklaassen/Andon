## 1. Decision Requested

Approve a dual-sourcing plan that moves a share of MAT-5001 (Valve Body Casting) and MAT-5002 (Pump Housing Casting) volume from SUP-1007 Brightline Castings to SUP-1002 Keystone Foundry, and authorizes evaluation of SUP-1003 Silesia Metalworks as a European source. **Decision requested from Priya Nair and Dana Whitfield by 2026-09-22.**

## 2. Background

Brightline Castings is the primary source for MAT-5001, MAT-5002, MAT-5004 and MAT-5005 under MSA-BLC-2023. Since the production change on 2026-05-01 (new furnace line and a new sand supplier), performance has deteriorated sharply:

| Metric | Before 2026-05-01 | After 2026-05-01 | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| On-time delivery | 92% | 44% | 95% |
| Average days late when late | n/a | 9.3 days | 0 |
| Incoming PPM | 237 | 2,726 | 500 (SQA-BLC-2023) |

SCAR-2026-014 was issued on 2026-05-25 and is tracked as CAPA-2026-101 (due 2026-07-24), which is overdue. Brightline's 8D response was late and covered containment only. Per SCR-2026-Q3, Brightline is rated Red.

## 3. Impact to Date

| Impact | Measure |
|---|---|
| Material-shortage downtime events on consuming lines (mainly GVL-L3, MTY-L3, CLB-L2, MTY-L1) | **214** events |
| Porosity non-conformance reports after May | 380 NCRs |
| Cost of poor quality (scrap, rework, handling) from porosity since May | approximately **$1.68M** |
| Premium freight and expedite costs | tracked separately; recoverable under MSA-BLC-2023 clause 4.2 |

These figures will continue to grow while Brightline remains the sole source for the four materials.

## 4. Options

### Option A – Remain single-source and enforce the contract
Rely on corrective action and chargebacks. Lowest immediate cost, but no protection if the permanent fix slips again. Not recommended as the only course of action.

### Option B – Dual-source MAT-5001 and MAT-5002 with Keystone (recommended)
Qualify Keystone Foundry (Erie, USA) as second source and shift volume in stages.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Source | SUP-1002 Keystone Foundry (PPM limit 500; Q3 FY26: 308 PPM, 94% on-time; FY26: 347 PPM, 97% on-time) |
| PPAP / qualification | approximately 12 weeks from tooling release |
| Piece-price impact | approximately +6% versus Brightline contract price |
| Target volume share at steady state | 40% Keystone / 60% Brightline (staged: 20% at PPAP completion, 40% after 8 weeks of stable performance) |
| Contract basis | MSA-BLC-2023 clause 5.2 permits reallocation of up to 50% without liability |

### Option C – Full resourcing away from Brightline
Moves 100% of volume. Highest price and qualification risk, and Keystone cannot absorb full volume of four materials in the near term. Held as contingency.

### Option D – Evaluate Silesia Metalworks (SUP-1003) for Europe
Silesia (Katowice, Poland; PPM limit 250; no FY26 receipt history, so performance is to be established) serves Wroclaw and the Rotterdam DC. This option is a feasibility study only: sample castings and a capacity review, with a go/no-go in Q1 FY27.

## 5. Cost-Benefit Summary (Option B)

| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Piece-price premium | +6% on the shifted share (about 40% of MAT-5001/5002 spend) |
| PPAP and tooling duplication | one-time, to be quoted by Keystone; budget placeholder in FY27 plan |
| Avoided cost of poor quality | the porosity cost since May is approximately $1.68M; halving exposure on two materials is expected to recover several multiples of the price premium |
| Avoided shortage events | 214 events to date; second source reduces single-point-of-failure risk |

The business case rests on the observation that the annualized price premium is a fraction of the $1.68M already incurred in a single period.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Keystone PPAP slips beyond 12 weeks | Weekly PPAP gate review; Rosa Martinez on site |
| Brightline reacts to volume loss by reducing service further | Retain 60% share; enforce chargebacks under clauses 4.1 and 4.2 |
| Interim shortages before Keystone is live | Raise safety stock for MAT-5001/5002 per POL-INV-001; premium freight authorized by Priya Nair |
| Keystone capacity constrained by MAT-5003 demand | Capacity confirmation required before award |
| Quality escape at new source | First-article inspection and Controlled Shipping Level 1 for first 3 lots |
