## Part 1 – Customer Letter

**Apex Agricultural Equipment – Supplier Management**
**Date:** 18 August 2026
**To:** Dana Whitfield, VP Operations, and Priya Nair, Director of Supply Chain & Logistics, KK's Parts
**From:** Rebecca Haines, Director of Supplier Management
**Subject:** Delivery performance escalation – KK's Parts, supplier record CUST-2001

Dear Ms. Whitfield and Ms. Nair,

Apex Agricultural Equipment values the partnership with KK's Parts as a Strategic supplier. I am writing to formally escalate a pattern of late deliveries that is now affecting our assembly schedules.

**1. Our requirement.** Under the Apex Supplier Scorecard, every Strategic supplier must achieve **95% On-Time, In-Full (OTIF)**, measured as delivered on or before the Apex-requested date and in the full ordered quantity. Failure to meet the standard for two consecutive quarters places a supplier on Apex's Supplier Improvement Plan and affects future sourcing awards.

**2. What we observe.** Our receiving data shows that KK's Parts' overall on-time performance to the requested date is **95.8%**, but measured as on-time **and in full**, overall OTIF is only **92.6%** — below our **95% OTIF** standard. The gap is concentrated in one carrier: shipments moved by **Ridgeway Freight** from your Memphis distribution center arrive on time to our requested date only **91.7%** of the time, compared with about **98%** for shipments carried by other carriers (on-time and in-full, the Ridgeway-carried shipments score 88.3% against 91.8% to 96.8% for the other four carriers). The shortfall is concentrated on the Ridgeway-carried shipments, several of which have arrived two to four days after the date we requested.

**3. Impact.** Late arrivals have caused two line-side shortages at our assembly plants and required Apex to pay for expedited inbound handling. The aggregate result is currently inside the standard, but with no margin. Any further deterioration on the Ridgeway lane will push KK's Parts below 95%.

**4. Request.** Please provide, by **2 September 2026**:

1. A root-cause analysis of the late Ridgeway-carried shipments.
2. A written corrective plan with dates and an accountable owner.
3. Weekly OTIF reporting for Apex orders until the plan is closed.
4. Confirmation of the carrier and routing that will be used for Apex shipments going forward.

I would welcome a call with your team this week. Thank you for your prompt attention.

Sincerely,
Rebecca Haines
Director of Supplier Management, Apex Agricultural Equipment

## Part 2 – KK's Parts Response Memo

**MEMORANDUM**
**Date:** 26 August 2026
**To:** Rebecca Haines, Apex Agricultural Equipment
**From:** Priya Nair, Director of Supply Chain & Logistics
**Cc:** Dana Whitfield; Brian Wilson, Logistics Manager, Memphis DC
**Subject:** Response to your letter of 18 August 2026 – corrective plan

Dear Ms. Haines,

Thank you for your letter. We have reviewed it with our Memphis distribution center team and agree with your data. We are sorry for the disruption to your assembly schedules.

### 1. Root cause

Our analysis confirms that the shortfall is carrier-driven. Ridgeway Freight (CAR-04) carries about 38% of Memphis shipments and has delivered 77% on time to its own carrier due date for the fiscal year (72% in August), against a 95% service-level requirement in our carrier agreement CTA-RF-2024-07. Contributing factors are peak-season congestion and a summer driver shortage at the carrier. Other carriers serving Memphis, including Summit Logistics (94.6%) and BlueLine Express (97.4%), are performing close to or above target. Our own warehouse processes have not contributed to the late deliveries.

### 2. Corrective actions

| # | Action | Owner | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Move all Apex (CUST-2001) shipments off Ridgeway to Summit Logistics (CAR-01) and BlueLine Express (CAR-02), by weight and lane | Brian Wilson | Effective 2026-09-01 |
| 2 | Reduce Ridgeway's overall share of Memphis volume from about 38% to no more than 15% | Priya Nair | 2026-11-01 |
| 3 | Claim contractual service credits and require a written corrective action plan from Ridgeway | Priya Nair | 2026-09-12 |
| 4 | Add a ship-date buffer check so any Apex order due within one day of the carrier due date is flagged to the Memphis Logistics Manager before release | Brian Wilson | 2026-09-08 |
| 5 | Provide weekly Apex OTIF reporting (on-time to your requested date, in full) | Brian Wilson | First report 2026-09-04, then weekly |
| 6 | Hold a joint review call with Apex Supplier Management | Priya Nair | Week of 2026-08-31 |

### 3. Expected result

By removing Apex orders from the Ridgeway lane, we expect your on-time performance to move toward the approximately 98% level seen on other carriers and your OTIF to hold above 95% with margin. Detailed carrier analysis is documented in CPR-MEM-2026-08. We will report progress to you every week until three consecutive weeks are at or above 95%.

### 4. Commitment

Dana Whitfield and I are accountable for this plan. Please contact me directly with any concern.

Sincerely,
Priya Nair
Director of Supply Chain & Logistics, KK's Parts
