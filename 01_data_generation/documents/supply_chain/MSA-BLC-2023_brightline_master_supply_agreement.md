This excerpt reproduces the operative commercial and delivery clauses of the Master Supply Agreement between KK's Parts ("KK's Parts") and Brightline Castings ("Supplier"). Quality requirements are governed separately by the Supplier Quality Agreement SQA-BLC-2023 and are referenced here only where they interact with delivery terms. Pricing schedules are omitted.

## 1. Scope of Supply

1.1 Supplier is KK's Parts' primary source for the following castings, released against blanket purchase orders:

| Material | Description | Primary consuming plants |
|---|---|---|
| MAT-5001 | Valve Body Casting | CLB, MTY |
| MAT-5002 | Pump Housing Casting | CLB, MTY |
| MAT-5004 | Manifold Casting | CLB, MTY |
| MAT-5005 | Bearing Cap Casting | CLB, MTY, GVL (selected part numbers) |

1.2 Gearbox Housing Casting (MAT-5003) is not covered by this Agreement; it is sourced from SUP-1002 Keystone Foundry.

1.3 Supplier ships to the plant warehouses WH-CLB (Columbus), WH-MTY (Monterrey) and, for selected bearing caps, WH-GVL (Greenville), on the incoterm stated in each purchase order.

## 2. Forecasts and Lead Times

2.1 KK's Parts provides a rolling 13-week forecast, refreshed weekly, and a firm 4-week order window. Quantities inside the firm window may not be reduced by more than 10% without Supplier consent.

2.2 Standard lead time from firm purchase order to receipt at the plant warehouse is 6 weeks for MAT-5001 and MAT-5002, and 7 weeks for MAT-5004 and MAT-5005.

2.3 Supplier shall confirm a promised delivery date for every purchase order line within 2 business days of release. The promised date as confirmed (or as last mutually agreed in writing) is the reference date for all delivery measurement.

2.4 Supplier shall give written notice within 1 business day of becoming aware of any event likely to delay a shipment beyond its promised date, including furnace, tooling, raw material or sand-supply changes.

## 3. Delivery Performance Standard

3.1 **On-time delivery definition.** A receipt is "on time" if the goods are received at the KK's Parts plant warehouse on or before the promised date. A receipt with zero days late is on time; any receipt after the promised date is late, and days late is measured in calendar days from the promised date to the actual receipt date.

3.2 **Target.** Supplier shall achieve on-time delivery of at least **95%** of receipts in each calendar month, calculated as receipts with zero days late divided by total receipts.

3.3 KK's Parts measures performance from its receiving records and publishes it monthly on the Supplier Scorecard (see SCR-2026-Q3). Supplier may dispute a measurement within 10 business days with documentary evidence of an agreed date change.

## 4. Late-Delivery Remedies

4.1 **Expedite at Supplier cost.** For any shipment received more than 2 calendar days late, Supplier shall, at its own expense, expedite the replacement or remaining quantity by the fastest reasonable mode.

4.2 **Premium freight chargeback.** Where KK's Parts incurs premium freight (air, team-driver, or expedited LTL/FTL) to protect production because of a late Supplier shipment, KK's Parts may charge back 100% of the incremental premium freight cost, supported by carrier invoices and the related shortage record.

4.3 **Corrective plan trigger.** If monthly on-time delivery falls below 95% for two consecutive months, Supplier shall deliver a written recovery plan within 10 business days, with weekly progress reporting until three consecutive months at or above target.

4.4 **Line-down costs.** Where late delivery causes a documented material-shortage stoppage at a KK's Parts line, KK's Parts may recover direct, documented costs of the stoppage (idle labor and overtime to recover) as set out in Schedule D, subject to the cap in clause 9.

4.5 Remedies in this clause are cumulative with, and not in substitution for, the quality remedies in SQA-BLC-2023 (including SCAR and 8D requirements).

## 5. Volume Commitment and Dual-Sourcing Rights

5.1 KK's Parts designates Supplier as primary source for the materials in clause 1.1 and will allocate not less than 70% of its annual requirement for each material to Supplier, subject to clauses 5.2 and 5.3.

5.2 **Performance-based reallocation.** If Supplier's monthly on-time delivery is below 85% in any two of three consecutive months, or if incoming defect rate exceeds the 500 PPM limit in SQA-BLC-2023 in any two of three consecutive months, KK's Parts may qualify one or more alternate sources and reallocate up to 50% of the affected material's volume without liability under clause 5.1.

5.3 Supplier shall cooperate with qualification of alternate sources, including supplying drawings, tooling data and first-article requirements under KK's Parts' PPAP process. KK's Parts-owned tooling remains KK's Parts' property and may be relocated on 30 days' notice.

## 6. Termination

6.1 **For cause.** KK's Parts may terminate this Agreement or any material schedule on 30 days' written notice if Supplier fails to cure a material breach (including sustained delivery or quality failure under clauses 3, 4 or 5.2) within the cure period stated in a written notice of breach. The cure period shall be 30 days unless extended by KK's Parts.

6.2 **Safety stock on termination.** On notice of termination, Supplier shall build and hold up to 6 weeks of finished safety stock for the affected materials at KK's Parts' request, invoiced at the contract price.

6.3 **Convenience.** Either party may terminate for convenience on 180 days' written notice, subject to completion of firm orders.

## 7. Business Reviews

7.1 The parties shall meet quarterly to review on-time delivery, quality (PPM), open SCARs, forecast accuracy and capacity. Open corrective actions referenced by SCAR or CAPA number (for example SCAR-2026-014 and CAPA-2026-101) are standing agenda items until closed.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 Recovery under clause 4.4 in any contract year is capped at the value of the purchase orders for the affected material delivered in the preceding 12 months.

## 10. Governing Law

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio, USA. Disputes are escalated first to the parties' executive sponsors (KK's Parts: Dana Whitfield) before any proceedings.
