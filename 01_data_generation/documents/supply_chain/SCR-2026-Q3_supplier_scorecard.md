## 1. Purpose and Method

This scorecard summarizes delivery and incoming-quality performance for KK's Parts' casting suppliers for Q3 of fiscal year 2026 (April to June 2026). Metrics follow the KK's Parts KPI definitions (see GLS-SCM-001):

- **Supplier OTD** = receipts with days late = 0 divided by total receipts. A receipt is on time when received on or before the promised date.
- **PPM** = rejected quantity divided by received quantity, multiplied by 1,000,000.

Status rules: **Green** = meets both on-time target and PPM limit; **Amber** = misses one by a narrow margin or recovers within the quarter; **Red** = misses on-time target or PPM limit materially, or has an overdue corrective action.

## 3. Brightline Castings (SUP-1007) – Flagged Red

### 3.1 Before and after 1 May 2026

| Metric | Before 2026-05-01 | After 2026-05-01 | Contract requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| On-time delivery | 92% | 44% | 95% (MSA-BLC-2023) |
| Average days late, when late | not material | 9.3 days | 0 |
| Incoming PPM | 237 | 2,726 | 500 (SQA-BLC-2023) |

Before 1 May, Brightline was slightly below the on-time target (92% vs 95%) but inside the PPM limit. After 1 May, both measures collapsed.

### 3.2 Root-cause signals

- Brightline moved production to a new furnace line and changed sand supplier at the start of May.
- Incoming inspection shows porosity as the dominant defect on MAT-5001 and MAT-5002.
- Late shipments are averaging 9.3 days beyond the promised date, which is driving material-shortage stops at GVL-L3, MTY-L3, CLB-L2 and MTY-L1.

### 3.3 Corrective actions

| Item | Reference | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier Corrective Action Request | SCAR-2026-014 (issued 2026-05-25) | Brightline 8D response late; containment only |
| Tracked corrective action | CAPA-2026-101 (due 2026-07-24) | Open, past due |
| Contract basis | SQA-BLC-2023: SCAR response within 10 business days; 8D within 30 days | Not met |

Brightline's quality manager, Hector Salinas, has committed to a permanent corrective action plan; none has been accepted as of the report date.

### 3.4 Recommendation

1. Maintain Brightline on **Red / Controlled Shipping Level 2** (100% sort at Brightline, certification with each lot).
2. Begin qualification of alternate sources. Candidates are **SUP-1002 Keystone Foundry** and, for European demand, **SUP-1003 Silesia Metalworks**. See PRP-SC-2026-04.
3. Invoke MSA-BLC-2023 clauses 4.1 and 4.2 (expedite and premium freight at Brightline's cost) on late lots.

## 4. Alternate Suppliers

### 4.1 Keystone Foundry (SUP-1002)

Keystone supplies Gearbox Housing Casting MAT-5003 and is just below target on delivery in Q3 (94%, against 100% in Q1 and 94% in Q2) and inside its 500 PPM limit (308 PPM in Q3, improving from 440 in Q1). Capacity for additional MAT-5001/5002 volume requires PPAP and a tooling duplication plan. Keystone has indicated approximately 12 weeks for PPAP and a piece-price premium of about 6%.

### 4.2 Silesia Metalworks (SUP-1003)

Silesia holds the tightest PPM limit of the three (250). It has no FY26 receipt history, so on-time and PPM performance are untested and would be established during qualification. Its location supports European demand served via the Rotterdam DC (DC-RTM) and the Wroclaw plant (WRO).
