## 1. Summary

Ridgeway Freight (CAR-04) delivered **72% on time in August 2026** against a contractual SLA of 95% in CTA-RF-2024-07. For the fiscal year to date, Ridgeway's on-time rate is **77%**, the lowest of the major LTL carriers and well below the 92% service-credit threshold. Ridgeway carries about **38%** of DC-MEM outbound shipments, so its performance is the largest single driver of late customer deliveries from Memphis. This review recommends shifting volume to Summit Logistics (CAR-01) and BlueLine Express (CAR-02) and invoking service credits.

## 2. Method

Carrier on-time = shipments delivered on or before the carrier due date, where carrier due date = ship date + planned transit days for the lane (see CTA-RF-2024-07 clause 3.2 and GLS-SCM-001). Data source: DC-MEM shipment records reconciled to carrier EDI 214 delivered events, with duplicate EDI messages removed per SOP-WMS-002.

## 7. Customer Impact

Late Ridgeway deliveries affect customer on-time-in-full performance. For CUST-2001 Apex Agricultural Equipment, on-time to the customer-requested date is 91.7% on Ridgeway-carried shipments versus about 98% on other carriers (95.8% on-time overall). Apex's OTIF — on time and in full — is about 92.6% overall and 88.3% on Ridgeway, below Apex's 95% OTIF requirement. See ESC-APEX-2026-08 for the customer escalation and response.

## 8. Recommendations

1. **Shift volume.** Reduce Ridgeway's share of DC-MEM shipments from approximately 38% to no more than 15% by 2026-11-01, moving LTL volume on Midwest and Southeast lanes to Summit Logistics (CAR-01) and small-package and light-weight volume to BlueLine Express (CAR-02). Prioritize all CUST-2001 shipments immediately.
2. **Claim credits.** Submit the July and August claim ($202,273) and request a schedule for earlier FY26 months.
3. **Corrective action plan.** Require a written plan from Ridgeway within 10 business days under CTA-RF-2024-07 clause 6.3.
4. **Reserve termination.** If September on-time remains below 92%, issue notice under clause 8.3 (repeated failure) or clause 8.2 (60 days' notice for convenience). No minimum volume commitment applies.
5. **Capacity check.** Confirm Summit and BlueLine have capacity for peak season before the transfer; Summit's FY26 on-time of 94.6% is within 0.4 point of target.
