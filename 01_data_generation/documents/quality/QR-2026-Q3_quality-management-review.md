## 2. Executive Summary

1. Supplier quality is the largest driver of cost of poor quality. Brightline Castings (SUP-1007) incoming PPM rose from 237 to 2,726 against a 500 PPM limit; related COPQ is approximately USD 1.68M since 2026-05-01.
2. Night-shift crimp defects at WRO-L1-WC02 are contained; permanent actions are partly complete, but the process data do not yet show recovery (C-shift defect rate 13.6%, Cpk 0.33 since 2026-06-08).
3. Greenville bore-out-of-tolerance NCRs rose sharply on GVL-L3 with the degrading spindle on GVL-L3-WC01.
4. All three storyline CAPAs (101, 102, 103) were behind plan at the date of this review.

## 6. Greenville Bore Out of Tolerance (CAPA-2026-103)

Bore out of tolerance NCRs on GVL-L3 have risen since March in step with the deterioration of the spindle on GVL-L3-WC01, CNC Horizontal Machining Center #1, which machines the gearbox bearing bores. Bore out of tolerance NCRs on GVL-L3 numbered 3 in March and then 10 (April), 14 (May), 10 (June), 24 (July), 15 (August) and 17 (September); Greenville logged 102 bore NCRs across FY26. The increase tracks the vibration trend recorded by maintenance (see PM-CNC-030 and MOC-2026-031) and indicates bearing bore position drift caused by spindle condition, not a material or operator issue.

Quality actions to date:
- 100% in-process bore checks on GVL-L3 at the CMM; Quality Hold events are logged in MES when drift is detected.
- Bore NCRs sorted and reworked where possible; scrap on late detection is a COPQ driver.
- The CAPA owner is the Greenville Plant Manager, Tom Brennan, with Luis Hernandez and Ashley Taylor.

The CAPA cannot be closed until the spindle is replaced and bore Cpk of at least 1.33 is demonstrated for three consecutive lots. CAPA-2026-103 has been past due since 2026-07-23 without an approved extension.

## 7. Supplier Quality

Per SQA-BLC-2023 Section 6, a late or incomplete SCAR response is a Level 2 trigger. The Director of Quality supports the recommendation in SCAR-2026-014 to move Brightline to Level 2 controlled shipping and to begin the chargeback process. Resourcing options are being reviewed by Supply Chain and Logistics.
