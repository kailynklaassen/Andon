## 1. Parties and Scope

This agreement is between KK's Parts and Brightline Castings, Saltillo, Mexico (SUP-1007). The Brightline quality contact is Hector Salinas, Quality Manager. It covers the following cast parts shipped to KK's Parts plants (principally CLB and MTY, and GVL for some parts):

| MAT-5002 | Pump Housing Casting |

| MAT-5005 | Bearing Cap Casting |

## 2. Performance Requirements

| Metric | Requirement | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Defect PPM | Not more than 500 PPM, measured monthly per supplier | Rejected quantity / received quantity x 1,000,000 (QP-7.4) |
| On-time delivery | At least 95% | Receipts with days_late = 0 / receipts |
| SCAR response | Within 10 business days of issue | Containment and initial root cause |
| 8D report | Within 30 days of SCAR issue | Complete 8D with permanent corrective action |
| Certificate of conformance | With every shipment | Heat/lot, furnace line, sand supplier identified |

## 3. Process Change Control

Brightline shall notify KK's Parts in writing at least 60 days before any change to furnace, melt practice, sand or binder supplier, tooling, gating or heat treatment. Changes require a PPAP resubmission at the level specified by KK's Parts (normally Level 3) before shipment of changed product. Shipments of product from an unnotified process change are treated as nonconforming.

## 4. Porosity Acceptance Criteria

Castings shall be free from porosity that affects pressure integrity, sealing surfaces or machined features.

1. **Radiographic inspection** (first-article, and each lot sampled per the control plan): evaluate to ASTM E155 reference radiographs. Critical zones (pressure-containing walls, port bosses, sealing faces) must meet Grade 1 (no worse than Level 1 gas porosity). Non-critical zones must meet Level 2.
2. **Machined-surface porosity:** on machined sealing surfaces, no pores with a diameter greater than 0.5 mm, and no more than 2 pores of 0.5 mm or smaller in any 25 mm diameter area. Porosity that breaks through a machined sealing surface is rejectable.
3. **Pressure/leak test:** castings designated pressure-retaining are tested at 1.5x working pressure with a hold of 30 seconds and shall show no visible leakage or pressure decay greater than the part specification.
4. **Sectioning:** 1 casting per furnace lot is sectioned per quarter and per any process change; no interconnected shrinkage porosity is permitted.
5. Impregnation or weld repair is not permitted without written KK's Parts approval.

## 5. Nonconformance, SCAR and Chargebacks

Where incoming inspection or in-process machining at KK's Parts finds castings nonconforming, KK's Parts opens an NCR (QP-8.7) and, if the PPM limit is exceeded or the defect is systemic, issues a SCAR. Brightline bears the following costs:

- Cost of replacement or credit for rejected castings and freight for returns.
- Sorting and rework labor at KK's Parts at the plant's burdened rate.
- Machining value added to a casting that is scrapped due to a hidden casting defect (porosity found after machining).
- An administrative fee of USD 350 per SCAR.
- Documented line-stoppage cost resulting from late or nonconforming shipments, where agreed in writing.

Chargebacks are invoiced monthly by Supply Chain and Logistics based on Quality-approved NCR costs.

## 6. Escalation Levels

| Level | Trigger | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1: SCAR | Monthly PPM above 500, or a systemic defect, or on-time below 95% for two consecutive months | Formal SCAR; containment in 10 business days; 8D in 30 days; weekly status call |
| Level 2: Controlled shipping | SCAR response late or incomplete, or PPM above 500 for 2 consecutive months after a SCAR, or 8D not accepted | Independent 100% inspection at Brightline (or third party) before shipment, with inspection records; KK's Parts on-site audit; weekly executive review; cost borne by Brightline |
| Level 3: New-business hold | Level 2 exited unsuccessfully after 60 days, repeated recurrence, or loss of confidence | No new part numbers or quotes awarded; resourcing plan (alternate sources) activated; formal notice from the Director of Supply Chain & Logistics |

Exit from a level requires 3 consecutive months at or under 500 PPM and on-time delivery of at least 95%, plus acceptance of the 8D by the Director of Quality.

## 7. Audit and Access

KK's Parts may audit Brightline's Saltillo plant with 5 business days notice. Brightline shall provide process and inspection records, radiographic films and sand test data on request.

## 8. Term

This agreement renews annually. Termination of the agreement does not relieve either party of obligations for product already shipped. The agreement is referenced in the supply contract for the materials in Section 1.
