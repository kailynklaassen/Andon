## 2. Reported Condition

KK's Parts reported repeated spindle alarms, bore position drift on finished gearbox parts, and an audible growl above 4,000 rpm. The machine had been taken offline by the Greenville team on 2026-08-15 after a spindle overload trip (WO-900308) and was run on a restricted basis until the Hartwell engineer arrived on 2026-08-19.

## 4. Diagnosis

Advanced wear of the front spindle bearing set with loss of preload and degraded grease. The vibration level of 7.6 mm/s is 3.1 mm/s over the replacement limit of 4.5 mm/s. KK's Parts' PM records show readings below KK's Parts' internal action limit of 7.1 mm/s in the months before the visit; the Hartwell limit is considerably lower and was exceeded for several months.

## 5. Work Performed

1. Replaced the drawbar clamping grease and cleaned the spindle taper.
2. Performed a temporary spindle relubrication and a partial run-in to restore limited operation.
3. Captured a baseline vibration spectrum for trending.
4. Re-checked bore position on a test part; machine released to production at reduced spindle speed (maximum 4,500 rpm).

This was a temporary measure. It does not correct the worn bearings or restore preload.

## 6. Recommendation

1. **Replace the complete spindle cartridge** (exchange unit SPN-CART). Quotation: $68,400 installed. Standard lead time is 10 weeks from purchase order. No spare is held at KK's Parts' Greenville site.
2. Until replacement, limit spindle speed to 4,500 rpm, measure vibration weekly and stop the machine if it rises above 9.0 mm/s.
3. Align KK's Parts' PM vibration limits with Table 7-2 (alert 3.5 mm/s, replace 4.5 mm/s).
4. Consider holding a spare cartridge for this A-critical machine.

## 7. Warranty Warning

Hartwell notes that this machine has been operated above 4.5 mm/s RMS. In accordance with section 7.7 of the service manual, **operation above 4.5 mm/s voids the spindle warranty** on the current cartridge and on consequential damage to the headstock. This visit is therefore invoiced as chargeable service. A replacement cartridge supplied on exchange will carry a new 12-month warranty provided Table 7-2 limits are observed.
